I am an **Assistant Professor** in the **Department of Electrical Engineering** at the  
**Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur**, India.

Prior to joining IIT Kharagpur, I was a **Postdoctoral Fellow** at **Michigan State University**, USA, and at the **Technion – Israel Institute of Technology**.

My research lies at the intersection of **optimal control**, **differential game theory**, and **path planning**, with applications to autonomous vehicles, surveillance, pursuit–evasion problems, and robotics.

---

### Research Interests
- Optimal control theory  
- Differential game theory  
- Path planning
